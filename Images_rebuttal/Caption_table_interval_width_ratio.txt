**Ratio Between Interval Lengths (Outliers vs. Inliers)**: This analysis compares the average interval length for outliers and inliers, with inliers defined as the 20% of data with the lowest LOF scores. Using the *Airfoil* and *Winiwite* datasets, interval estimates are generated by EPICSCORE with MDN, GP, and BART, as well as UACQR-P/S and CatBoost-based quantiles as base model. EPICSCORE-MDN achieves the lowest ratio on *Winiwite*, while on *Airfoil*, both MDN and BART reach the highest ratios, highlighting MDN’s strong performance across settings.
