**Ratio Between Interval Lengths (Outliers vs. Inliers)**: This table compares the average prediction interval length for outliers versus inliers, where inliers are defined as the 20% of samples with the lowest LOF scores. Results are shown for the *Airfoil* and *Winewhite* datasets using EPICSCORE with MDN, GP, and BART, along with UACQR-P/S and CQR and CQR-r. On *Winewhite*, EPICSCORE-MDN achieves the highest ratio, while on *Airfoil*, both the MDN and BART variants lead, underscoring MDN’s strong adaptability to varying data densities.
