HPD vs EPICSORE: Comparison of prediction regions generated by the HPD (Highest Posterior Density) and EPICSCORE methods for a challenging scenario with high epistemic and aleatory uncertainty. The experiment considers simulated data composed of high- and low-density regions (80% of the samples uniformly distributed in \([-1, 0]\) and 20% following a Beta(5, 5) distribution in \([0, 1]\)), with a conditional mean given by a step function (\( \mathbb{1}_{x > 0} \)) and heteroscedastic variance defined by \( \sigma(x) = 0.05 + \sin^2(15x)\mathbb{1}_{x > 0} \). The base model is an MDN with 1-64-128-5 architecture (input-layers-components), trained on 2000 points. EPICSCORE uses BART (50 trees, gamma density errors, heteroscedastic variance) calibrated on 1000 posterior samples, while HPD derives from the MDN. Both methods perform comparably in high-density regions, but EPICSCORE provides sharper uncertainty quantification in sparse regions (e.g., \(x > 0\)).
